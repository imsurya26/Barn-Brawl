## Visual Bible (Apply to All Clips)

**GAME:** Barn Brawl
**GENRE:** Browser-based multiplayer 3D arcade physics sports
**STYLE:** Stylized, colorful, fun, polished, farm-themed, slightly chaotic, competitive
**CHARACTERS:** Goat, Pig, Cow, Chicken (four distinct cartoon farm animals as player characters)
**CORE OBJECT:** Large stylized rock (approximately 3 metres diameter) used as the sports ball
**ENVIRONMENT:** Large enclosed farm-themed sports stadiums with crowds, goal posts, floodlights, boost pads, and powerup pickups on the turf
**TONE:** Fun, fast, chaotic, energetic, competitive
**COLOR PALETTE:** Warm greens, earth tones, and vivid team colors — OR shift to night/snowy/canyon palette per map
**ART DIRECTION:** Stylized 3D animation, clean geometry, expressive animal characters, no photorealism

**AVOID:**
- Photorealistic rendering
- Human football/soccer players
- Cars or vehicles
- Weapons or violence
- Dark horror tone
- Rocket League branding or assets
- Copyrighted logos or mascots
- Trying to render readable URLs or complex typography inside the video
