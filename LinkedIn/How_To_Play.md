# How To Play — Barn Brawl

## Quick Start

1. Open **https://barn-brawl.vercel.app/** in a desktop browser (Chrome or Edge recommended)
2. Enter your nickname
3. Click **Create Room** (host) or **Join Room** (enter a room code from the host)
4. Choose your **animal**, **boost style**, and (host only) your **map**
5. Mark yourself **Ready**
6. When all players are ready, the host clicks **Start Match**
7. Hit the rock into the opponent's goal to score
8. Most goals before the timer wins. Tied at full time? Sudden-death overtime!

> **Note:** The game server runs on a free host and may take ~60 seconds to wake from sleep on first visit. Refresh or reconnect if needed.

## Controls

| Key / Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **W** | Accelerate forward; pitch nose down in air |
| **S** | Brake / reverse; pitch nose up in air |
| **A** | Steer left; yaw left in air |
| **D** | Steer right; yaw right in air |
| **Space** | Jump (hold for extra height) |
| **Space × 2** | Double-jump (press again while airborne) |
| **Space + direction (in air)** | Aerial dodge / directional flip |
| **Shift** | Boost (drains boost meter, recharges passively) |
| **Z** | Air roll left |
| **C** | Air roll right |
| **Left Ctrl** | Powerslide (maintains forward momentum while turning) |
| **Q** | Toggle **Rock Cam** (camera follows the rock) / Player Cam |
| **R** | Reset follow camera behind your animal |
| **Mouse drag** | Orbit camera around your animal |
| **Scroll wheel** | Zoom camera in/out |
| **E** | Use your carried **powerup** |
| **F** | Use your animal's **special ability** |

---

## Boost

- Your boost meter fills from 0 to 100
- Hold **Shift** to boost — costs 33.3 units/second
- Boost regens at 12 units/second when not active
- Pick up **small boost pads** (+12) or **large boost pads** (full refill) scattered around the arena
- Each animal has a cosmetic boost style (visual only, same physics)

## Animal Abilities (F key)

| Animal | Ability | Cooldown | Effect |
|--------|---------|----------|---------|
| **Goat** | Ram Rush | 12 s | Charge forward; your next rock hit is empowered |
| **Pig** | Belly Bounce | 14 s | Hop and push nearby rivals on landing |
| **Cow** | Ground Stomp | 15 s | Grounded pulse pushes rivals and the rock outward |
| **Chicken** | Wing Burst | 12 s | Brief upward lift with stronger aerial control |

---

## Powerups (E key)

Collectable items spawn at fixed points around the field. Walk over one to carry it; press **E** to activate.

| Powerup | Duration | Effect |
|---------|----------|--------|
| **Power Shot** ✨ | 10 s | Your next rock strike hits significantly harder |
| **Magnet Pulse** ∞ | 2 s | Briefly pulls the nearby rock toward you |
| **Shield** ◟ | 3 s | Reduces incoming knockback from rivals |
| **Speed Surge** ϙ | 2 s | Burst of extra acceleration |
| **Shockwave** ◎ | 0.4 s | Short-range pulse pushes nearby rivals and the rock |

You can only carry one powerup at a time.

---

## Scoring

- **Goal:** Push the entire rock through the opponent's goal — +1 to your team's score
- **Assist:** Last team-mate to touch the rock before a goal is awarded an assist
- **Save:** Stop a rock that was heading into your own goal
- Match lasts 5 minutes. Tied? Sudden-death overtime — next goal wins.
- After the match: see full stats (goals, assists, saves, shots, powerups, abilities used)

---

## Camera Tips

- Use **Q** to toggle Rock Cam when the ball is far away
- Use **R** to quickly snap the camera back behind your animal
- Scroll to zoom out for a wider view in busy situations
- Camera, graphics, accessibility, and audio settings are accessible in-game via the settings panel

## Multiplayer Tips

- Coordinate with team-mates — two animals hitting the rock together creates stronger shots
- Save your boost for critical defensive moments or a clear goal shot
- Use Goat's Ram Rush just before contact with the rock for a powerful strike
- Chicken's Wing Burst excels for aerial challenges when the rock is high
- Powerslide around the rock to quickly reposition without losing too much speed

---

## Limitations

- Keyboard and mouse only — no gamepad or touch support yet
- Free server hosting may sleep when inactive (first-connect delay)
- Best experience on desktop Chrome or Edge at 1080p
