# Barn Brawl

The look is a sun-faded farm sports club: warm paper, dark forest ink, harvest orange and pond teal. Haymakers wear orange; Trailblazers wear teal. The arena and all four animals are original procedural geometry.

Color: paper #f6f2df, ink #263d32, orange #e77646, teal #69b8ba. Typography: Barlow Condensed for the club wordmark, scoreboard and headlines; DM Sans for controls and body text. Fonts have local generic fallbacks.

Layout: an editorial left menu over a live farm diorama; a compact central scoreboard and corner boost meter during play. Controls have firm borders, small labels and restrained motion. Clear keyboard focus, ready states, errors, empty slots, disconnect feedback and disabled host controls are part of the interface.

Geometry and effects stay lightweight: material-batched static scenery, low-poly dynamic animals, one spherical rock collider, and a fixed particle pool. Cosmetics never enter physics calculations.
